# YouTube Video Skills

Build channel intros, outros, and podcast audiograms.

## Overview

The complete two-Skill iart.ai package combines branded YouTube opening and closing sequences with audiograms that turn podcast audio into video.

## Scenarios

- Create a channel intro or outro
- Turn a podcast segment into an audiogram
- Reuse episode audio in branded video

## Usage

Provide the channel identity, audio, duration, and target format. Coordinate graphic timing with sound and check captions and exports for the intended platform.
