# Structured Thinking Skills

Choose and apply explicit reasoning procedures for decisions and difficult problems.

## Overview

The catalog covers diagnosis, systems, uncertainty, risk, strategy, innovation, and execution through models such as first principles, scientific testing, OODA, and pre-mortems.

## Scenarios

- Diagnose causes and compare competing explanations
- Evaluate a risky or reversible decision
- Develop strategy and test an idea

## Usage

Provide the decision and its evidence, then use the model router to select a fitting frame or combination. Follow each model’s procedure and preserve its assumptions and output.
