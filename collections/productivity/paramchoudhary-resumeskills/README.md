# Resume and Career Skills

Prepare truthful resumes, applications, interviews, and career decisions.

## Overview

The collection covers ATS review, role tailoring, achievement bullets, cover letters, LinkedIn, portfolios, academic and executive resumes, interviews, and offer negotiation.

## Scenarios

- Tailor a resume to a job description
- Prepare interview stories and application materials
- Compare offers and plan negotiation

## Usage

Provide the real resume, job posting, and career context. Work through analysis, tailored materials, and interview preparation while preserving the accuracy of your experience.
