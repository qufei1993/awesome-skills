# Mechanical Engineering Research Skills

Develop thermal-fluid research with domain analysis, writing, data, and review workflows.

## Overview

This nine-Skill suite combines mechanical-engineering guidance with thermal-fluid analysis, proposals, literature writing, data analysis, slides, schematics, mentoring, and reviewer-author revision.

## Scenarios

- Check thermal-fluid assumptions and validation
- Prepare research proposals and manuscripts
- Analyze results and present research

## Usage

Provide the research question, governing assumptions, and evidence. Choose the specialist workflow and preserve the upstream references and scripts for domain checks and editable outputs.
