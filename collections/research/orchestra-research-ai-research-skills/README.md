# Orchestra AI Research Skills

Carry AI research from model experiments through evaluation and publication.

## Overview

Orchestra Research’s native categories cover model architectures, training, alignment, inference, infrastructure, interpretability, agents, RAG, and research writing.

## Scenarios

- Design and run model training experiments
- Evaluate and serve models
- Develop research ideas and paper artifacts

## Usage

Use the upstream installer or category plugin. Provide the research environment, datasets, and compute access, and keep each category’s reference resources available.
