# UnifAPI Marketing Agents

Research marketing decisions using live public data through UnifAPI.

## Overview

The author’s role, industry, and platform agents cover SEO, AI visibility, competitors, influencers, local businesses, and social listening. Their workflows research and estimate rather than publish posts.

## Scenarios

- Audit search and AI visibility
- Research competitors and influencer pricing

## Usage

Connect UnifAPI’s MCP server and complete OAuth, then install the marketing Skills. Choose the role or industry agent matching the business question.
