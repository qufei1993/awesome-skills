# Understand Anything Skills

Explore codebases and knowledge bases through interactive knowledge graphs.

## Overview

Understand Anything maps files, functions, dependencies, and business domains into a graph, with Skills for analysis, explanation, changes, onboarding, and graph conversations.

## Scenarios

- Map an unfamiliar codebase before changing it
- Explain a change and its affected dependencies
- Explore a wiki or business domain visually

## Usage

Use the upstream plugin or platform installer, analyze the project, then open its dashboard. Keep the plugin’s analysis pipeline and dashboard resources available.
