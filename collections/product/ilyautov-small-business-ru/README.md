# Small Business RU Skills

Support Russian small-business operations with local finance, tax, customer, and staffing workflows.

![Small Business RU Skills — original project cover](<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilyautov/small-business-ru/43ad4add8a4d242332d1e6753339771bbeb26401/assets/social-preview.png>)

## Overview

The author’s thirty-four-Skill package adapts small-business tasks to Russian operations, including simplified-system taxes, civil-law contracts, employment, cash flow, sales, and counterparty checks.

## Scenarios

- Review cash flow and operating finances
- Prepare customer and staffing workflows
- Check counterparties and business documents

## Usage

Provide the business facts and relevant reporting period. Follow upstream setup for computation and registry access; retain source dates when using legal or tax material.
