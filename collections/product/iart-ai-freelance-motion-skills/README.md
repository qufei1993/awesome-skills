# Freelance Motion Skills

Manage motion-design briefs, pricing, revisions, delivery, and brand standards.

![Freelance Motion Skills — original project cover](<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/iart-ai/freelance-motion-skills/d85c2b484693d0387333c778d9c95230fa1856ce/showcase.gif>)

## Overview

This five-Skill iart.ai package supports the business side of freelance motion work, from clarifying client requests to defensible quotes, revision boundaries, delivery specifications, and motion guidelines.

## Scenarios

- Clarify a client brief and scope
- Prepare quotes and manage revisions
- Document delivery and brand motion standards

## Usage

Provide the client request, deliverables, schedule, and brand material. Establish scope and revision terms before production, then check the final delivery specifications.
