# dontbesilent Business Skills

Diagnose business and content problems and turn them into concrete next actions.

## Overview

dontbesilent’s Chinese toolbox supports business judgment, content systems, decisions, delegation, learning, and reusable local records.

## Scenarios

- Diagnose an offer, customer, or pricing problem
- Improve content and organize practical follow-up

## Usage

Bring the current problem and its materials to the dbs router. Use save and restore for continuity; the separate public tweet archive is not part of Skill installation.
