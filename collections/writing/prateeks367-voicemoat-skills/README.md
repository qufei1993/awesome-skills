# VoiceMoat Skills

Write LinkedIn and X content in a consistent voice and review it before publishing.

## Overview

VoiceMoat’s complete twenty-six-Skill catalog covers drafts, hooks, endings, stories, profiles, comments, replies, content pillars, repurposing, planning, team posting, and performance reflection.

## Scenarios

- Draft and revise posts in your voice
- Plan content and reply workflows
- Review publishing quality and results

## Usage

Provide writing samples, the idea, and platform context. Use the free Skills directly; optional VoiceMoat integration adds a voice profile trained from published posts.
